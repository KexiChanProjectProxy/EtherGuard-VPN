test(e2e): edge failover, bootstrap fallback and revision reset across supers

Also fixes a genuine flake: clusterSession.readRecord used the injected logical/test clock for the OS socket read deadline; under frozen e2e clocks the computed deadline could already be in the past, causing spurious ErrClusterLinkDead and endless reconnect churn. Now uses real wall-clock time for the socket deadline while keeping injected time for logical cluster state. Regression test: TestClusterSessionStaleLogicalClockKeepsSocketDeadlineAlive.

Ultraworked with [Sisyphus](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent)

Co-authored-by: Sisyphus <clio-agent@sisyphuslabs.ai>
